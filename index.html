    Frequently Asked Questions

    Guidance on accounts, listings, advertising, and getting started

    The answers below outline how buyers and sellers use the platform. For questions about a specific listing, payment, event, or seller, contact the relevant party directly to confirm current details.

    Who can use Africa Market Place?

    Small businesses, entrepreneurs, independent professionals, and buyers. Whether you sell products, provide services, recruit talent, or look for quality offerings, there is a place for you on the platform.

    How does the social media promotion work?

    We feature your business on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok through professionally produced reels, stories, and posts, extending your reach to prospective customers who might not otherwise discover you.

    What can I list in the marketplace?

    Goods such as clothing, electronics, artwork, household items, food products, and accessories, plus services, jobs, housing, autos, and events. Each listing can include photographs, a description, and pricing. Browse the full listing categories to find the right fit.

    How do buyers contact me?

    Interested buyers find your listings and connect with you directly through the platform, so inquiries and orders come straight to you.

    Can I use advertising and selling separately?

    Yes. Advertising and selling are independent services. Use either one on its own, or combine them so that social media promotion directs prospective buyers to your listings.

    How do I choose the right listing category?

    Select the category that best matches your offering, so relevant buyers find it where they already search. If you are unsure, contact our team and we will help you place your listing appropriately.

    How quickly can I get started?

    In minutes. Create your business profile, publish your first listing with a photograph and price, and your offering is ready to be discovered.
