    Frequently Asked Questions

    Quick answers before you get started

    Who can use Africa Market Place?+

    Small businesses, entrepreneurs, independent sellers, and buyers. Whether you sell products, offer services, or look for great deals, there is a place for you.

    How does the social media promotion work?+

    We feature your business across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok through reels, stories, and posts, so more potential customers discover what you offer.

    What can I list in the marketplace?+

    Clothing, electronics, artwork, household items, food products, accessories, and other goods, plus services, jobs, housing, autos, and events. Each listing can include photos, descriptions, and prices.

    How do buyers contact me?+

    Interested buyers discover your listings and connect with you directly through the platform, so inquiries and orders come straight to you.

    How quickly can I get started?+

    In minutes. Create your profile, add your first listing with a photo and price, and you are ready to be discovered.
